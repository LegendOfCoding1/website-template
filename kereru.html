About Kererū

Kererū is a great option for a family, and it is perfect for families with 2-4 children. It has 2 bedrooms and a separate living area, and it can fit up to 2 adults and 4 children.

Like all of our chalets, Kererū is on the mountain, so you can ski to and from the door.

Features

  • Great option for a family
  • 2 bedrooms
  • Perfect for families with 2-4 children
  • Separate living area

Booking

Only Club members can book. Bookings must be from Friday to Sunday. The chalet is on the mountain, so you can ski to and from it.
