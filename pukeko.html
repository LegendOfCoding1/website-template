About Pūkeko

Pūkeko is a great choice for large groups and families who want a chalet to themselves. It can fit up to 6 adults and 15 children.

Limits on the number of adults and children make sure everyone has a comfortable stay. Like all of our chalets, Pūkeko is on the mountain, so you can ski to and from the door.

Features

  • Great for large groups/families wanting a chalet to themselves
  • Limits on adult/child number ensure a comfortable stay
  • On the mountain: ski to and from the chalet

Booking

Only Club members can book. Bookings must be from Friday to Sunday. The chalet is on the mountain, so you can ski to and from it.
