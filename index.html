Welcome to Maunga Club

Maunga Club hires snow/ski chalets (cabins) on the mountain to Club members. Join the Club to enjoy a weekend away in the snow.

About the Club

Maunga Club gives its members a place to stay right on the mountain. We have three chalets, each with its own features, and they offer shared accommodation for between 6 and 40 people. Whether you are coming with a big group or just your family, there is a chalet to suit you.

Things to do on the mountain

Skiing and snowboarding

The mountain gives 550 hectares of skiing and snowboarding area.

A long season

It has the longest ski/snowboard season in New Zealand.

Ski to your door

All three chalets are on the mountain, so you can ski to and from your chalet.

Booking rules

  • Only Club members can use the chalets.
  • All bookings must be from Friday to Sunday.

How to join

To join as a Club member, email Maunga Club the following:

  • Full name
  • Physical address
  • Proof of address (for example, a copy of a power bill)
  • Proof of ID (driver's licence or passport)

Send it to maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub. Your membership will be confirmed by email. We will post you a membership letter with more information about the Club and the accommodation.
