Welcome the Maunga Club

Maunga Club hires snow/ski chalets (cabins) on the mountain to club members. Join the Club to enjoy a weekend away in the snow.

Things to do on the Mountain

  • skiing and snowboarding on 550 hectares of snow
  • The longest ski/snowboard season in New Zealand
  • Ski to and from your chaet, because all three cahlets are on the mountain

Our Chalets

Our chalets offer shared for between 6 and 40 people.

Chatlets 1: Kākāpō

Maximum 10 adults and 30 children. the largest of the chalets.

See the Kākāpō chalet

Chalet 2: Pūkeko

Maximum 6 adults and 15 children. Great for large groups or families.

See the Pūkeko chalet

Chalet 3: Kererū

Maximum 2 adults and 4 children. A great option for a family.

See the Kererū chalet

Booking rules

  • Only Club members can use the chalets.
  • All bookings must be from Friday to Sunday.
