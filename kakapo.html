About Kākāpō

Kākāpō is the largest of the three Maunga Club chalets. It offers shared accommodation for up to 10 adults and 30 children, which makes it a great choice for big groups and big families.

The large kitchen, dining and living area gives everyone room to cook, eat and relax together after a day on the mountain. Like all of our chalets, Kākāpō is on the mountain, so you can ski to and from the door.

Features

  • Largest of the chalets
  • Large kitchen, dining and living area
  • Great amenities
  • On the mountain: ski to and from the chalet

Booking

Only Club members can book. Bookings must be from Friday to Sunday. The chalet is on the mountain, so you can ski to and from it.
